# Buffer strategy

This project put reliability over performance.
It would be better to delete whole message than passing corrupted data.
A corrupted data may cause unexpected device behavior or deceiving report.


## CDC Rx Buffer

```mermaid
block
  columns 5
  space  space  RX[("<br>APP - CDC Rx Text<br>FIFO")]  space  space
```

## CAN Tx Buffer

```mermaid
block
  columns 6
  space  FR[("<br>APP - Tx Frame<br>FIFO")]  space  TX[("<br>HAL - Tx Frame<br>FIFO")]  space  EV[("<br>HAL - Tx Event<br>FIFO")]
```
```mermaid
block
  columns 6

  space                              FR_63  space  space  space  space
  blockArrowId1<["Tx Frame"]>(right) FR_62  space  space  space  space
  space                              FR_61  space  space  space  space
  space                              ...    space  space  space  space
  space                              FR_04  space  space  space  space
  id5>"Send Pointer"]                FR_03  space  TX_02  space  space
  space                              FR_02  space  TX_01  space  EV_02
  space                              FR_01  space  TX_00  space  EV_01
  blockArrowId2<["Tx Report"]>(left) FR_00  space  space  space  EV_00

  FR_03 -- "Copied" --> TX_02
  TX_00 -- "ACKed" --> EV_01
  EV_00 -- "Compare Marker" --> FR_00
```
